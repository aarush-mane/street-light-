How the numbers work. Power (W) = rated 100 W × brightness ÷ 100 for every lit pole, sampled once per simulated minute. Energy (kWh) = W × hours ÷ 1000. Cost = kWh × tariff. Baseline = a conventional dusk-to-dawn system: all 12 lights at 100% during the configured on-window, no dimming. Savings = baseline − actual for the same period and can be negative if lights run outside the schedule.
